DSH-2Xray ships this DLL only for its opt-in TUN mode on Windows. It copies
the file next to the xray executable at runtime; TUN is disabled by default.
If you prefer not to use the bundled copy, place your own wintun.dll beside
xray.exe, or disable TUN in the plugin settings.
